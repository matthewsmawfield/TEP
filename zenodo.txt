This paper proposes a covariant, testable reformulation of relativity in which proper time is a dynamical field and the "speed of light" is an emergent, strictly local invariant rather than a global constant. The framework is built on a single spacetime manifold with two metrics: a gravitational metric g_μν and a causal (matter) metric g̃_μν to which all non-gravitational fields and clocks couple. In isotropic shorthand, g̃_μν = A²(φ) g_μν + B(φ) ∇_μφ ∇_νφ, where φ is the time field, A(φ) = exp(β_A φ/M_Pl), and B(φ) encodes tiny light-cone deformations consistent with GW170817 (|c_γ - c_g|/c ≲ few×10⁻¹⁵); decomposed along the unit cosmic temporal flow U_μ ≡ -∇_μφ̄/√(2X̄) (g^μνU_μ U_ν = -1, spatial projector Π_μν = g_μν + U_μ U_ν), this resolves into g̃_μν = -A_loc²(u_grav) U_μ U_ν + Aₛₚ²(u_drift) Π_μν + B(φ)(Π_μ^αΠ_ν^β∇_αφ∇_βφ - 2(U·∇φ)U_(μΠ_ν)^α∇_αφ), separating the stationary baryon-sourced ADM lapse N_TEP(x) = A_loc(u_grav(x))N_GR(x) from the matter-frame ruler factor Aₛₚ(u_drift(t,x)) = exp(-u_drift(t,x)) on static 3-space γ_ij(x). All matter, electromagnetism, and quantum phases evolve with respect to g̃-proper time τ, guaranteeing local Lorentz invariance and locally invariant c while permitting path-dependent global synchronization. Cosmologically, TEP adopts an eternal, spatially infinite, inhomogeneous background without physical expansion. Neither space nor matter changes size: γ_ij is static while Aₛₚ recalibrates matter-frame units, governing the intergalactic ruler ratio 1+z = Aₛₚ(tₒ)/Aₛₚ(tₑ) integrated in flight across unscreened space. Noncompact solutions demonstrate an on-shell inhomogeneous temporal landscape with near-zero native matter curvature (|Ω_k^native| = 1.40× 10⁻⁴), sustained roll, and positive-definite fluctuation spectra across the canonical (λ_0 = +0.413 > 0, λ_1 = +39.1) and jointly on-shell two-branch (λ_0 = +0.563 > 0, λ_1 = +555.4) sectors, bounded below by the Bloch gap λ ≥ min m_eff² > 0 on a positive CMC initial-slice lapse; the master potential V(u) = V_matter(u)e^-(u/u_s)⁴ + V₀ e^-(u_s/u)⁴ matches the reconstructed profile to ∼ 10⁻¹⁶, with early-universe/background closure developed quantitatively in Papers 18, 26, 27, 29, 30.

Long-standing confusions about "variable c" are resolved by replacing convention-dependent statements with invariant observables. A synchronization one-form σ̃ on spacelike slices yields a GR-subtracted curl dσ̃ defining the residual "temporal holonomy" H_resid, which vanishes in GR and in the purely conformal subclass and is nonzero only when non-exact transport (B(φ) ≠ 0) is present. Embedding the preferred-foliation geometry in a spatially covariant ADM/khronon action establishes four results: (i) electromagnetic waves and gravitons share the principal wave operator □_TEP ≡ -(N_TEPc)⁻²∂_t² + Aₛₚ⁻²(t,x)γ^kl∂_k∂_l, enforcing c_GW(t,x) = c_γ(t,x) everywhere (Δ t_γ–GW ≡ 0 at B=0); (ii) the static-space WKB tensor measure √Aₛₚ⁺¹· Aₛₚ⁻¹ ≡ 1 eliminates FLRW Hubble friction, preserving 1/r graviton decay, the standard-siren discriminator Ξ(z) = d_L^GW/d_L^EM = 1/(1+z), and the inverse detector chirp-mass map ℳ_det = ℳ̃/(1+z) at pinned interior response; (iii) a static φ-gradient produces zero propagation asymmetry in the purely conformal limit; and (iv) the two-branch kinetic sector (P_,X = k√(ξ) + 2ξ, k≃ 16 fixed by g_t and the empirical scale a₀ ∼ cH₀, zero per-system fit parameters) screens Solar-System PPN and preferred-frame observables (|γ_PPN-1|≃ 4.32× 10⁻⁸ and |α_2|≃ 2.16× 10⁻⁸ < 2.4× 10⁻⁷ at 1.6 R_⊙, |α_1|≃ 5.86× 10⁻⁵ < 10⁻⁴ at 1 AU), lunar laser ranging (|Ġ_loc/G_loc|_LLR≲ 1.7× 10⁻¹⁸ yr⁻¹), and pulsar dipole radiation while passing the wide-binary test at 0.4–1.7σ and generating flat rotation curves and unstretched-space lensing via A_loc(u_grav), whereas loop holonomy scales with B₀ (|H_resid|≃ 4.33× 10⁻¹³ |B₀| s per loop in an Earth–Sun–satellite triangle). Six experiments are outlined: satellite-triangle holonomy (< 10⁻¹⁸), portable-clock anholonomy (10⁻¹⁹), multi-species annual clock modulations (10⁻¹⁹–10⁻¹⁷), interplanetary optical links, altitude-screening maps, and multi-messenger tests.

Einstein's postulate of universal c was a brilliant, operationally perfect approximation in regimes where time's flow is effectively uniform. In a universe where the rate of time is dynamical yet locally Lorentzian, "the speed of light" emerges as an invariant in every local lab but ceases to be globally universal. The new invariant content resides in path-dependent synchronization defects and holonomies of time transport, not in naive one-way "speeds." If detected, these invariants would inaugurate a post-Einsteinian era: from dynamic geometry to dynamic time.
